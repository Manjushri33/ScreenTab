## Design reference: Windhawk Alt+Tab per monitor

The Alt+Tab filtering strategy and relevant Windows shell symbol names were informed by the open-source `Alt+Tab per monitor` Windhawk mod by L3r0y and the Windhawk project by Ramen Software. ScreenTab is a separate implementation and does not bundle Windhawk.

Windhawk: https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk
Windhawk mods: https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk-mods
